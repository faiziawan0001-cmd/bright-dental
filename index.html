About Dr. Ayesha Khan

Dr. Khan has over 10 years of clinical experience and is committed to providing the absolute best in quality, thorough and gentle dental care for every patient. She treats her patients like family and is especially skilled in working with children, nervous patients and complex procedures.

She completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery with high honours, followed by an MSc in restorative and cosmetic dentistry. Since then she has pursued advanced training in dental implants, endodontics (painless root canal treatment) and full smile makeovers — taking pride in treating thousands of patients with care and precision.

Where It All Began

Bright Smiles was founded with a single mission: to make high-quality dental care accessible, comfortable and genuinely painless for families in Lahore. Born from Dr. Khan's passion for transforming smiles and her dedication to patient welfare, the clinic was designed to feel welcoming from the ground up.

Our rooms are bright and quiet, with state-of-the-art equipment, rigorous sterilisation standards and a team trained in modern dentistry — so you always feel respected, informed and completely at ease.

Today, with a 5.0-star Google rating and thousands of patients treated, Bright Smiles stands among the city's most trusted dental practices. We remain as devoted as ever to every patient who walks through our door.
